HOW TO PLAY

Both games share the same loop. Nothing to install: click Play, then pick New Game. Progress saves automatically in your browser, so you can close the tab and pick Continue later.

  1. Take a ticketWalk to HQ and press E. Pick a ticket. Green, yellow and red set how much time you get and how much it pays.
  2. Get to the siteFollow the yellow arrow. The SLA timer (top right) is already running, and enemies on the map can steal time or Tech$$$.
  3. Fix itPress E at the site's door to start that ticket's mini-game. Each one explains its rules on the intro card.
  4. Get paidFinish fast for up to +50%. A blown SLA pays half. Failing costs one of your 3 lives and the ticket stays open.
  5. Level upSpend T$ at the Supply Depot. Cert scrolls unlock new ticket types. Lifetime earnings raise your rank, which opens new areas and bosses.

TIPS FOR NEW HIRES

Getting started

  • Take green tickets close to HQ first. They're forgiving and pay out fast.
  • Buy a cert scroll early: the new ticket types pay 2-5x more.
  • Three clean closes in a row (no fails, no DNS) earns a +25% streak bonus.
  • Losing all 3 lives means burnout and costs 10% of your T$. Bailing with Esc is free.

Later on

  • Buy the Golden Label Maker / Runbook well before the Auditor. It only documents sites you close after buying it.
  • The AI Drone (500 T$) has 3 charges. Save them for boss phases you hate.
  • Loading screens show real networking and sysadmin tips, so the games double as light training.
  • Each game keeps a local leaderboard. Beat the final boss to sign it.
